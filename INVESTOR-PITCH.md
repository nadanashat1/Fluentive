# Fluentive CRM - Investor Pitch Deck

## 🚀 Company Overview

**Fluentive** is an AI-powered CRM and Sales Operating System designed for small and mid-sized businesses to close more deals faster.

---

## 📊 The Problem

### Sales Teams Are Struggling
- **73%** of leads never get contacted
- **42%** of sales time is spent on non-selling activities
- Average sales rep uses **6+ tools** that don't talk to each other
- Small teams can't afford Salesforce but need enterprise features

### The Gap in Market
- **Salesforce**: Too expensive ($75+/user), too complex
- **HubSpot**: Expensive at scale, limited AI features
- **Spreadsheets**: No automation, no insights
- **Small CRMs**: Lack AI, team features, and growth tools
